<p><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: HelveticaNeue; color: rgb(51, 51, 51);">Walgreens is extremely excited to announce the integration of GoToPills with the Walgreens Prescription APIs. Checkout the integration with the GoToPills </span><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: HelveticaNeue;"><a href="https://apps.apple.com/ee/app/gotopills/id1150966568"><span style="color: #FF0000;">iPhone app</span></a></span><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: HelveticaNeue; color: rgb(255, 0, 0);"> </span><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: HelveticaNeue; color: rgb(51, 51, 51);">or </span><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: HelveticaNeue;"><a href="http://https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gotopills_reactnative&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US"><span style="color: #FF0000;">Android app</span></a><span style="color: #FF0000;"> </span></span><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: HelveticaNeue; color: rgb(51, 51, 51);">where customers can easily refill or transfer a Walgreens prescription.</span></p>
<p><span>Along with the convenience of refilling Walgreens prescriptions, GoToPills delivers cutting-edge technology to customers </span><span style="font-size: 11.000000pt; font-family: 'HelveticaNeue';">with “off-label” alerts when a medication is not approved by the FDA for the prescribed reason, age, gender, or duration. By partnering with Walgreens, customers now have access to GoToPills’ vital drug summaries including FDA approved uses, major adverse effects, and litigation levels along with hyperlinks to the supporting medical research that is linked to each summary. </span></p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: HelveticaNeue; font-size: 11pt;">“Integrating the GoToPills app with the Walgreens Prescription Refill and Transfer Program provides customers with easy prescription refill convenience coupled with vital medication information that increases patient safety. We could not ask for a better win-win solution for customers” said Tamera </span><span style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: HelveticaNeue; font-size: 11pt;">Venzke, Co-Founder and CEO of GoToPills.</span></p>
