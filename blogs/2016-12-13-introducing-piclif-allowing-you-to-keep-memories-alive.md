Walgreens is excited to introduce PICLIF, a new Photo Prints API integration. The app can be found on the Apple App Store , Google Play Store , and their Web site . The PICLIF ( https://piclif.com/ ) mobile app and Web site allows families and funeral homes to display pictures and memories of a loved one during a funeral service and to easily print the photos at Walgreens.
