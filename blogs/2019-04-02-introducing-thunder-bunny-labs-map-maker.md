<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p>We wanted to hop back onto our blog to introduce <a href="https://www.thunderbunnylabs.com" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Thunder Bunny Labs</a> a new integration of our Photo Prints API. They have launched their new <a href="https://www.thunderbunnylabs.com/mapmaker" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Map Maker</a> feature which helps you build an amazing photo map of the 50 US states on their website with your photos inside the frame of any state!</p>
<p>With the 50 States Photo Map from Thunder Bunny Labs you can create a one-of-a-kind conversation piece using your own photos from around the USA. Their innovative design allows you to fill the map over time, so whether you are a lifelong traveler or just beginning your adventure you will always remember where you are going and where you have been. Filling up your map is now easier than ever thanks to integration with the Walgreens Photo Prints API. Simply upload your photos into the virtual map, adjust the size and position if you wish, and have them printed directly to a Walgreens near you. The prints will fit perfectly into your map so you can spend less time worrying about cropping and print settings and more time planning your next vacation!</p>
<p>Hop on over to start building your Photo Map of the 50 US States today on the <a href="https://www.thunderbunnylabs.com/mapmaker" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Thunder Bunny Labs Map Maker</a> website.</p>
